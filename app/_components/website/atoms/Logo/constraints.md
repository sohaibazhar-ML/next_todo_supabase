## Dimensional Specifications
- **Header Swisscom**: Defined by the header organism ($40px$ height standard).
- **Footer Swisscom**: Defined by the footer organism ($32px$ height standard for secondary branding).
- **Partner Logos**: Standardized through parent molecules (e.g., $h-12$).
